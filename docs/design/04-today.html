Design note

One grid, one card, one form per fact. Twelve columns with a 24px gutter, in the rows a director acts in: the hero at 12 — whatever needs the director this morning, in the product’s own order of urgency, here the money held by six approvals with who holds them under it and how many are older than a week; four tiles at 3·3·3·3 — margin at risk, payables this week, overdue receivables by how long overdue, unsigned variations — each the disc left of a one-line title, the figure at 24, one line of meaning, a bar where there is a ratio and its one action at the foot; the money owed each way at 6·6, the bar in ageing buckets and the buckets printed beneath; money in and out at 8 beside cash by account at 4, absent until Tally is linked and saying so in product words; ordered against contract at 6 beside milestones at 6, absent because no stage carries a date; site today beside your day; spend by trade package beside the pipeline. Cards in a row share the row’s height; no card sits alone; the last row is full. Under 1380px the tiles go two a row, under 1000px 8·4 becomes 12 and 12, under 760px everything is one column. A panel whose module is off is not drawn and its row re-flows — the third sample below. Two projects are past their contract because the product sums every order gross: KRA-01 at 127% and SAN-01 at 104% — the seed shapes SAN-01’s orders to 88% of the contract before GST, and no project sits at the watch line once GST is in.

Where the seed does not reach, the panel says so in the product’s words and nothing is estimated. No report is dated today and no stage carries a date; the seed records no sent date on a variation and no expected close date on a lead, so oldest and closing this month are not printed. The gaps are recorded in VALUE-MAP and the README, never on the screen: a product frame carries no marker, no gate’s name and no note to ourselves — the frame gate reads every one.

Design note

Inside a project, Today becomes Overview on the same grid: one hero — the thing on this project that needs you — then four tiles: ordered and billed against the contract, margin at risk on this project, the variations awaiting the client’s signature, payables this week on this project; then the agreement’s stages, undated, so milestones this week is absent in product words, beside this site’s week; then money in and out for this project at 8 beside the team at 4. One form per fact here too: the variation the client is sitting on is the tile, not a list as well; what Today answers at the firm level is not repeated with the same number — on the seed every bill is this project’s, so its payables coincide with the firm’s, which is the seed and not a repeat. The sidebar is the project’s; the square raises an order on it without asking which.

Design note

One colour unless the data is a status. The bars take the brand chart colour and the hovered bar its hovered step; the figure is in the tooltip beside the bar, never on it, and the sentence under the chart says the same numbers for anyone who cannot see it. The health bar is the status series in the owner’s order — off track, at risk, on track, paused — in the status chart colours, with a hairline of the surface between segments as the guidance asks; off track is hatched and paused stippled, so the two problem states are told apart without colour, and the legend carries the counts. The status series is the bold step: the base success and warning chart colours measure 2.44:1 and 2.47:1 on the light card, under the 3:1 the non-text gate holds a chart mark to, so the whole series takes the bold step and stays one family.

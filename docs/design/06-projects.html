Design note

A missing cost rate stays blank and the Cost stat says what to do: 5 lines have no cost rate, and names them. Nothing derives a cost from the client rate, and the cost total is marked partial until every line carries one. An order raised from the BOQ copies the cost rate and refuses a line without one.

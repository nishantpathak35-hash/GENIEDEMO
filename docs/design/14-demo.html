14The five-minute demo

Six screens, one story, about five minutes, on the demo organisation — Bhitarang Interiors Private Limited, exactly as the seed writes it — and the bar says so, so nobody types a real order into it. The story crosses the two levels once: the firm’s Today, then one project, and everything after that is that project’s. The people are the seed’s logins: Shalini Kamath at the firm, Bidisha Sen at Prakashvahini Electrical Contracts on her phone, Elizabeth Kuriakose at Anuvanshik Life Sciences on her tablet. Under each screen is the one sentence the salesperson says. Screens do not close deals; a walkthrough does.

6

And this is how you get paid for it

Site › Measurement. The person certifying measures what was actually built this period; the running-account bill is raised against those quantities, not against an estimate. Anything measured beyond the contract quantity is marked in the error colour and blocks the bill until a variation covers it.

Why this order

The same seed throughout: ANU-01’s figures are the ones on Today and in part 4, and the walkthrough is performable on the seeded product with the seed’s own logins — the order it raises takes the next number the series gives, PO-0019. The order total and the variation amount are formatted by the same function as everything else in the file. Payments are not in the demo on purpose. Every tax figure on them rests on a rate a chartered accountant has not verified yet, and a demo should not end on a voucher marked Draft: provisional rates. The measurement sheet is the one stop the seed does not reach: its contract quantities are the seed’s BOQ, its claims the design’s.

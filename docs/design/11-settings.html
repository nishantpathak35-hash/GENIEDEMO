Review your tax settings

About two minutes. The rates are the ones every Indian business uses; three questions are about you.

Every rate here is provisional

Payments, challans, 26Q content, Tally vouchers and tax invoices compute with these rates and are marked as drafts on provisional rates. Unless drafts are switched on they are refused, until a chartered accountant’s name, membership number, firm and date promote each row.

Three questions about your business
Do your projects bundle materials and labour under one contract?

Most fit-out contracts do. This sets how GST is applied to your client invoices.

Do you pay transporters who have given you a PAN declaration?

Transporters with ten or fewer vehicles can declare, and then no TDS is deducted from their bills.

Was your turnover above ₹10,00,00,000.00 last financial year?

Above it, you deduct tax when you buy goods from a vendor (section 194Q).

What your answers change

Which of the rates on the left apply to your payments and your invoices. Money computes with them already; every statutory output stays a draft on provisional rates until a chartered accountant promotes each row. You can come back here any time.

Design note

The reframe. Statutory rates are the same for every Indian business. They are verified once, centrally, and ship pre-filled; the tenant reviews, answers three questions about their own business, and marks the review done. Until the chartered accountant’s details arrive every row is provisional and the screen says so first, in the caution notice — that is ADR-0014’s addendum of 15 September. The values shown are illustrative and tagged as such: the build seeds them from the spec, never from this file.

Use single-key shortcuts

/ and ? now type themselves like any other key. Search is still the first thing after the skip link, and the list of shortcuts is in your account menu.

Where it is

In the account menu, not in Settings

Settings are the firm’s and an administrator’s. This one belongs to the person: the person’s avatar on the top bar opens Your preferences, and the same menu lists every shortcut whether single keys are on or off.

Design note

WCAG 2.1.4, Character Key Shortcuts. A shortcut made of one printable character must be possible to turn off, remap, or limit to when its control has focus. / and ? stay, because staff who live in the product use them all day; this switch is how a person using speech input — where saying a word types its letters — or a switch device stops them firing. Only single characters are affected. Esc, the arrow keys inside a table and anything with Ctrl or Alt are not character keys and keep working. The product has no preference store today, so this screen needs one; it is in the list of what cannot be built yet.

  • A bar is at most 24px thick with a 4px rounded data-end and a square baseline; a group of two sits with a 2px surface gap between them.
  • A line is 2px with round joins; a marker is 8px or more with a 2px surface ring; the area under a line is the lightest step of the series’ own ramp (--chart-area, color.background.accent.blue.subtlest) — the token layer has no alpha step, and a fill at 10% would be a literal.
  • A gridline is a hairline one step off the surface, solid; the axis one step bolder; ticks in compact rupees — lakhs and crores, one decimal at most — with Indian grouping, the full figure in the tooltip and the table.
  • A 2px surface gap between stacked segments and touching bars; never a border around a mark; text wears text tokens, never a series colour.
  • A legend when there are two or more series, none for one; a table view behind a toggle on every chart that has an axis; on bars and dots a tooltip on the mark under the pointer, its hit target the whole column; on a line or an area a crosshair through the point under the pointer with the tooltip at it.
  • One y-axis, never two. Two measures of different scale are two charts.
  • A mark whose value is printed beside it is labelled-redundant and takes the fixed status amber (the gate grants it by finding the value in the card); a mark that is the only carrier of its value is held to 3:1.

As published

Every chart in the set is one class system — .chart with its kind — and every screen references this page. The published guidance is about colour: the title in the text colour, tick labels subtle, gridlines the border colour, one mark colour (the brand chart colour) unless the data is a status, a hairline of the surface between adjacent chart colours, no text on a chart colour, and the data in words as well. The status series takes the bold step because the base success and warning steps measure 2.44:1 and 2.47:1 on the light card, under the 3:1 the non-text gate holds every chart mark to; off track is hatched and paused stippled so the series survives a colour deficiency.

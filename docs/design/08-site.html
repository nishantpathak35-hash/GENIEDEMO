8Site

Site is four tabs on one nav item: the daily log filed from a phone, the imprest ledger, the measurement sheet that every RA bill is raised from, and the recce that starts a proposal. The seed files a report a day for five days on four sites, by the finance login — it has no site engineer, and no imprest, measurement or recce at all; those three screens are drawn on the seed’s names with the design’s own figures, and say so.

Design note

Imprest is site petty cash, not a statutory computation, so its figures are shown — the CA-gated surfaces are TDS, payments, billing and retention. A line without a receipt says so in words; it is not hidden and not a zero.
